# The idea bank

Fifty-four things ODD can post that are true, on-system, and not an
advertisement. Each one says what it is, what it needs before it can be made,
and which rendered format in `examples/` it uses.

The bank exists so nobody has to invent a post under pressure. Pick from it,
plan twelve at a time (see `README.md`), and shoot what you are missing.

**The two rules that outrank everything in this file**

1. **Never put a name on a face the source does not name.** A crowd photograph
   captions the night, not the people in it. Only a portrait that came to us as
   that person's portrait gets a name on it (format 19).
2. **Credit the photographer.** The archive's terms are editorial use with
   "ODDfest / photographer's name". The examples say "Photo: ODDfest" because
   the generator does not know who shot which frame. Replace it before posting.

## Formats beyond the single tile

- **Carousel** (`carousel-1…4`) — frame 1 states, 2–3 show, 4 asks. One night, one
  process, one argument. Each frame still has to stand alone: nobody swipes.
- **Story** (`story-1…3`) — doors, the ask, the credit. A story is a message, not
  a poster; one line each.
- **Highlight covers** (`highlight-1…6`) — the lockup suffix alone on Ink: FEST,
  FERENCE, SPACE, STUDIO, ARCHIVE, OPEN CALLS. They make the profile's top row
  read as one system.
- **Reel cover** — a title card in the grid style, so a video does not punch a
  hole in the grid. Use `post-15`'s layout and keep the first frame still.

## Planning a month

Twelve tiles, in this proportion:

- **six photographs** (A and B), of which at least two are people, not stages
- **three statements or questions** (D)
- **one lineup or programme card** (C)
- **one ask** (E) — and only if there is something real to ask for
- **one place or rhythm post** (F)

Paper on a diagonal: tiles 3, 5 and 10 of the twelve. Never two Paper tiles
side by side, never a Paper column.

## What would break it

Type on a photograph without a scrim · a sixth type size · Forta setting a
paragraph · an accent as a background · stock photography · a carousel that
needs swiping to make sense · announcing something undecided · a caption
written by somebody who was not there · a face with a name we did not ask for ·
an uncredited photographer.
