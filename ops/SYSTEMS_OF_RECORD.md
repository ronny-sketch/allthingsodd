## Explicit non-duplication rules (guide §2, carried over verbatim because

they're still exactly correct for this repo)

- Notion is never the CRM.
- beehiiv is never used as a CRM.
- MCP is never the production sync engine — see `ops/ARCHITECTURE.md`'s
  control-plane/data-plane section.
- A ticket buyer or newsletter subscriber never automatically becomes an
  Attio record — only an explicit B2B/B2G relationship reason promotes them
  (guide §25.2).
