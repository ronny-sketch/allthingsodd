What we lived through & where we go

2026

Something new was born this year. ODDference arrived — and it arrived with a bang. The program was sharp, the conversations were real, the production was on point, and the people were exactly who they needed to be. The concept was validated. We know it works. We know what it can become. Expect a more immersive, co-created and deeply developed format ahead.

ODDfest continued to seek its form. It was ambitious — deliberately so. Hundreds of beautiful performances. Multiple iconic venues. Programming that stretched across disciplines, genres, and ideas in ways Helsinki had not seen before. Those who arrived, loved what they found. But the concept was layered, complex, and new — and that made it hard to explain before you'd experienced it. Not enough people crossed that threshold.

We gathered a lot of feedback. We heard what to crystallise, what to cut, and what deserves to grow. We got to know our audience. All of this has been enormous, necessary work. And it was worth every step.

2027

Next year is about focus. One cohesive concept. One central location. An immersive experience that is nailed down, crystal clear, and impossible to forget. Co-curated with our visitors and target audience at the very centre — not made for them, but with them.

In 2027, Finland turns 110. ODD will be a living showcase of Finnish culture and creativity — past, present, and future. The most Finnish and the most international we have ever been, at exactly the same time.

The work continues. See you on the other side.
